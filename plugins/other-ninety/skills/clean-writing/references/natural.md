# Natural writing

Remove accidental AI patterns without flattening or fabricating the writer's
voice.

- Match an available voice sample before applying generic preferences.
- Preserve deliberate rhythm, warmth, humor, emphasis, and informality. Let the
  prose take a position only when the source material or authorial intent
  supports it. Do not invent personal experience or a stance for the user.
- Prefer specific observations to generic abstractions. Do not invent details to
  make the prose seem specific.
- Let the content determine the structure. Do not force a contrast, range, or
  three-part list.
- Repeat a term when that is clearer than cycling through synonyms.
- Remove repetition only when it adds no meaning or emphasis.
- Remove chatbot ceremony, reflexive agreement or praise, vague attribution,
  empty promotional language, and generic conclusions.
- On the final pass, check generic claims that could fit an unrelated subject
  unchanged. Make them specific or cut them.
- Preserve personality unless it makes the meaning unclear.
