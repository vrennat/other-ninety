# Clean writing

Read exactly one guide:

- `references/controlled.md` for procedures, runbooks, warnings, requirements,
  error messages, and acceptance criteria.
- `references/technical.md` for documentation, reports, explanations, notices,
  pull requests, and ordinary professional writing.
- `references/natural.md` for email, resumes, personal-site copy, proposals,
  essays, marketing, and other work where personal voice matters.

Start with meaning and cadence, not a banned-word or punctuation scan. Surface
patterns are clues, not proof, unless the author or style guide makes them hard
constraints. A sentence can avoid every common pattern and still sound generic.

Preserve the facts, intent, uncertainty, obligations, names, dates, numbers, and
links. Do not invent mechanisms, metrics, rates, or impact categories to make a
claim more specific. Flag the missing detail instead. After editing, check that
no condition, exception, action, or meaningful qualification was lost.
